Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Pulso, CNPJ (Brazil). You can send requests about your data to the email shown in the footer.

2. What data we collect

  • Account: name, email, WhatsApp number (optional) and password, which we store only in protected form (hash).
  • Video content: texts, colors, logo, product photo and the business contact details you fill in.
  • Payment: payments are made on Stripe. We only receive the status, amount, payment method and payer email. We do not receive or store card data.
  • Site usage: IP address and technical logs, for security and fraud prevention.

3. Why we use it

  • To create, render and deliver your video and to run your account (performance of the contract).
  • To meet legal and tax obligations, such as keeping sales records.
  • To protect the service against abuse and fraud and to fix problems (legitimate interest).

4. Who we share it with

  • Stripe, to process the payment.
  • Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI: when you ask for an AI script, the business form data (name, industry, what you sell, differentiators and offer) is sent to write the text.
  • Hosting provider, where the site and files are stored.

Some of these providers are located outside your country, which means an international transfer of data, made to deliver the service you bought. We do not sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Videos, logos and photos: up to 30 days after the video is ready.
  • Your saved brand (name, colors, style, contacts and the logo from your last video), so your next video can start with it: while your account exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Account data: while the account exists.
  • Payment records: for the period required by tax law.

6. Your rights

Under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and, where applicable, other laws such as the GDPR, you can ask for confirmation of processing, access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, and information about sharing. Just write to our contact email.

7. Cookies

We use only one essential cookie, which keeps you signed in to your account. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

8. Security

The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), passwords are stored in protected form, and access to data is restricted.

9. Minors

Pulso is intended for businesses and for people aged 18 and over.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. When that happens, the date at the top of this page changes.
